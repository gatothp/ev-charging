💡 Key formulas

Energy to add = Battery capacity × (Target − Start) / 100

Grid energy = Energy to add / Efficiency

Charging time = Grid energy / Charging power

Weekly driving energy = Distance per week / Vehicle efficiency

Weekly grid energy = Weekly driving energy / Efficiency

Cost = Grid energy × Price per kWh

ⓘ Notes

  • Charging time assumes constant charging power.
  • Actual power may vary, especially with DC fast charging.
  • Weekly driving calculation estimates the electricity needed to replace the energy used for driving.
  • Vehicle efficiency varies with speed, temperature, traffic, terrain and driving style.
  • PLN tariff examples are editable and should be checked against the current applicable tariff.
