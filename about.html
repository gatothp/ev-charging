About EV Charging Calculator

The EV Charging Calculator is a simple yet powerful tool designed to help electric vehicle owners and enthusiasts estimate their charging requirements, costs, and energy consumption patterns. Whether you're planning your weekly driving routine or calculating the cost of a single charging session, this calculator provides quick and accurate estimates based on your vehicle's specifications and local electricity rates.

Features

  • Weekly charging and energy calculations
  • Real-time charging cost estimation
  • Support for multiple EV models (Chery Q, BYD Atto 1, BYD M6, GAC Aion V, Geely EX2, Jaecoo J5, Wuling Air EV)
  • Custom vehicle specifications for any EV model
  • Flexible charging power input (kW or Ampere)
  • Indonesian and English language support
  • Mobile-friendly responsive design
  • 100% client-side calculation - no data is sent to any server

How It Works

Weekly Charging Tab: Calculate your weekly energy requirements based on your vehicle's specifications and driving distance. Understand how many charging sessions you'll need per week.

Charging Cost Tab: Estimate the time and cost of a single charging session. Enter your charging power, battery capacity, target charge level, and electricity rate to get precise calculations.

Important Notes

  • All calculations are performed locally on your device - no data is transmitted to any server
  • Charging time assumes constant charging power; actual power may vary, especially with DC fast charging
  • Vehicle efficiency varies with speed, temperature, traffic, terrain, and driving style
  • PLN tariff examples provided are for reference and should be verified against your current applicable rates
  • Charging efficiency accounts for losses between grid electricity and energy stored in the battery
